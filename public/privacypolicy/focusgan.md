# Privacy Policy for FocusGan!

**Effective date:** August 17, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

FocusGan! ("FocusGan!," "we," "our," or "the app") is a Pomodoro timer designed to help you focus, track completed sessions, and optionally block distracting apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, why it is needed, and how it is handled.

## Summary

- FocusGan! does not require an account.
- FocusGan! does not sell personal information.
- Your timer settings, blocked-app selections, and focus history are stored locally on your device.
- FocusGan! does not upload your focus history or installed-app list to our servers.
- Information is shared with another app only when you deliberately use a sharing feature.

## Information stored on your device

FocusGan! stores the following information locally:

- Focus and break interval settings
- Automatic interval preferences
- Theme and language preferences
- Selected apps to block during focus sessions
- Completed Pomodoro session history, including duration and completion time
- Current focus-blocking state

This information remains on your device and is used only to provide the app's features. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

## Installed-app information

On Android, FocusGan! accesses the list of launchable apps installed on your device so you can choose which apps to block during a focus session.

- The list is displayed and processed locally.
- FocusGan! stores only the package identifiers of apps you select.
- The installed-app list is not uploaded, sold, or used for advertising or profiling.

## Accessibility Service

On Android, app blocking uses the system Accessibility Service. This permission is optional but required if you enable distraction blocking.

While a focus interval is running, the service checks which app is in the foreground. If the foreground app is one you selected, FocusGan! displays a blocking screen.

FocusGan! does not use Accessibility access to:

- Read or collect text you type
- Read messages, passwords, or other screen content
- Perform clicks or gestures on your behalf
- Record the screen
- Upload Accessibility data
- Circumvent Android privacy or security controls

You can revoke Accessibility access at any time in your Android system settings. App blocking will stop working after access is revoked.

## Notifications

FocusGan! may request permission to send local system notifications when a focus or break interval finishes. Notification scheduling and delivery are performed on your device. FocusGan! does not use notifications for advertising.

You can disable notifications in your device settings, although timer completion alerts may no longer appear.

## Photos and media

When you choose **Save image**, FocusGan! requests the system access necessary to save your Pomodoro result image to your photo library or gallery. The app creates and saves only the result image you requested.

FocusGan! does not scan, upload, modify, or collect other photos or videos in your library.

## Instagram Stories and sharing

When you choose to share a result to Instagram Stories, FocusGan! generates the selected result image locally and sends it to the Instagram app through the operating system's sharing interface.

Sharing is initiated only by you. After the image is transferred, Instagram's handling of that image is governed by Instagram's own terms and privacy policy. FocusGan! does not automatically publish content or receive your Instagram credentials.

## Internet access and external content

The core timer, history, settings, and blocking features do not require a FocusGan! account or cloud service. The app may access the internet when you open externally hosted pages, including the Privacy Policy screen, website, or social links. Those website operators or hosting providers may process standard connection information such as your IP address and browser or device details under their own policies.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

FocusGan! does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent personal information.

## Data sharing

FocusGan! does not share locally stored data with us or third parties, except when:

- You deliberately share a generated result image with Instagram or another destination supported by your device.
- Disclosure is required to comply with applicable law or a valid legal process.

## Data security

FocusGan! relies on the security controls provided by Android, iOS, and your device. Although we design the app to keep its data local, no storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protecting your device with a passcode and keeping its operating system updated can reduce risk.

## Data retention and deletion

Focus session history and preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall FocusGan!. Images saved to your gallery are separate files and remain there until you delete them through your photo or gallery app.

## Children's privacy

FocusGan! is a general productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

You can:

- Decline or revoke Accessibility access and use FocusGan! without app blocking.
- Decline or revoke notification permission.
- Decline photo-library access and continue using the timer without saving result images.
- Choose whether to share any result image.
- Clear locally stored data through your device settings or uninstall the app.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality or legal requirements change. We will revise the effective date and make the updated policy available with the app or on the official FocusGan! website.
